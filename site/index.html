Shop bots for WooCommerce

Nyxify runs Telegram, Bale and Rubika bots for WooCommerce shops: customers browse the shop's products, fill a cart and order inside the messenger, and pay on the shop's own website; the people the shop chooses manage its products from the bot. A shop connects with the Nyxify plugin for WordPress and a license key.
